Privacy policy

Lamda_Cloud is used only by its owner, to back up the owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive.

  • It requests only the drive.file permission: it can see and change only the files it created itself, nothing else in the Drive.
  • Backups are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload.
  • No data is collected, shared, sold or sent anywhere other than the owner's Google Drive. There are no analytics and no cookies.
  • Access can be removed at any time in the Google account's security settings (third-party access).

Last updated: 2026-10-08.
